How to play

  1. Player 1 goes first and can play in any square of any board.
  2. The square you pick sends your opponent to the matching board. Play the top right square of any board, and your opponent must play in the top right board.
  3. Get three in a row on a small board to win that board. The side board shows it with your letter.
  4. If you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you may play in any open board.
  5. Win three boards in a row (across, down, or diagonal) to win the game.
